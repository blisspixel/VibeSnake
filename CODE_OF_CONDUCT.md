# Code of Conduct

Vibe Snake is built for curious players and thoughtful contributors. Project
spaces must remain safe, constructive, and focused on making the game better.

## Expected conduct

- Treat people with respect, including when reviewing ideas or code critically.
- Discuss observable behavior, evidence, and trade-offs rather than attacking a
  person or assuming intent.
- Welcome different experience levels, communication styles, identities, and
  accessibility needs.
- Protect private information and report security concerns through the private
  process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- Accept moderation decisions made to protect the project and its participants.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, discriminatory language, deliberate
disruption, doxxing, and sustained personal attacks are not permitted in project
spaces or in public conduct representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

A private conduct-reporting route is not configured yet. Do not post a conduct
report in a public issue. External contributions and official community spaces
must remain closed until a maintainer publishes and tests a concrete confidential
route here. Once that route exists, reports should include links or screenshots
when safe to do so. Reports will be reviewed with care for privacy,
proportionality, and the safety of affected people. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, restrict participation, or ban a participant when needed. A person
handling a report must recuse themselves if they are involved.

This policy applies to repository activity, release channels, and other spaces
officially operated for Vibe Snake.

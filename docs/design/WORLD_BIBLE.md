# World and Broadcast Bible

This document defines the foundation canon, narrative grammar, station identities, rival framing, and progression fiction for Vibe Snake. It describes intended 1.0 content, not current implementation status. [Current status](../release/STATUS.md) remains the authority for what exists in a playable build.

## Narrative purpose

The world must make every run feel like a transmission from a culture built around movement, rhythm, mutation, and public performance. It exists to deepen attachment and surprise without competing with the board.

The central fiction is:

> In the post-Molt world, music is infrastructure. Signal-serpents enter the Coil to turn precise movement into a living broadcast. Every route leaves an echo. Every recovery changes the story of the run.

The world supports the game in four ways:

1. It gives the radio a reason to exist beyond a playlist menu.
2. It turns AI policies into recognizable rivals and channels.
3. It gives cosmetics, achievements, replays, and challenges meaningful context without granting power.
4. It makes escalation and recovery feel authored while leaving every critical rule understandable without lore.

## Canon priority

When fiction and play compete, use this order:

1. Control, danger, and accessibility clarity.
2. Stable rules and score identity.
3. The run's emotional arc.
4. Character and station continuity.
5. Optional deep lore.

No joke, host line, visual flourish, or reveal may hide a fatal cell, mask a critical cue, delay input, change a scored rule, or become required to understand play.

## The world

### The Great Molt

The Great Molt was the point at which serpentine biology, memory, and broadcast technology became one shared signal ecology. It was neither a clean revolution nor a single invention. Old skins became archives, sensory rhythm became data, and the infrastructure that regulated heat and movement began carrying culture.

The current era is called post-Molt because every institution defines itself by what it believes the Molt meant. Some see coordination, some liberation, some competitive transcendence, and some a bureaucratic problem that must be made reassuring.

### The Coil

The Coil is both the public arena network and the cultural circuit built around it. A run is a regulated transmission through a finite movement lattice. The audience can watch live channels, revisit deterministic echoes, or enter the same seed under the same rules.

The Coil is not a death sport. A failed run is a broken transmission, and restart is a retune. This keeps the tone intense without making ordinary failure grotesque. Exceptional recoveries matter because they preserve a signal that was about to collapse.

### Signal-serpents

The player is a signal-serpent whose body carries the visible history of the current transmission. Growth records successful routing. A shed defines outward identity. A replay preserves the command history as an echo. No profile begins with stronger survival rules than another profile.

Signal-serpents are citizens, artists, athletes, technicians, archivists, and broadcasters. They are not human personalities with snake jokes pasted onto them. Their language grows naturally from heat, vibration, scale, shedding, coils, venom, signal, rhythm, and spatial memory.

## Gameplay-to-world mapping

Player-facing rule names remain direct. Fiction adds meaning without renaming a mechanic when that would reduce clarity.

| Game element | World meaning | Presentation rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grid | A finite broadcast lattice inside the Coil | Grid readability always outranks environmental detail |
| Food | A stable signal pulse that extends the transmission | It remains visually unmistakable and is never hidden behind lore terminology |
| Growth | The run accumulating visible signal history | Cosmetics cannot obscure head, body order, or occupied cells |
| Starvation | Signal decay when the route stops finding pulses | Urgency is communicated directly, not through fictional inference |
| Combo | Sustained resonance between routing and signal capture | Combo is the sole source of positive presentation escalation |
| Vibe Level | The broadcast's current resonance tier | One typed director owns every intensity transition |
| Power-up | A sanctioned temporary mutation in the signal body | Type, lifetime, trade-off, and consumption remain explicit |
| Near miss | A close pass recognized by the circuit | It celebrates intentional danger without overriding fatal warnings |
| Death | Transmission collapse by a named cause | Cause is attributable through at least two practical channels |
| Recovery | A stored mutation preserving a collapsing signal | Availability and consumption are visible before the save occurs |
| Restart | Retuning the same pilot for another transmission | Fast, deliberate, and protected from accidental confirmation |
| Replay | A deterministic echo of a completed or interrupted run | Rules, seed, version, and verification state remain visible |
| AI channel | A rival signal-serpent's public broadcast | AI follows the same declared rules and never alters human progress |
| Cosmetic loadout | A chosen shed and broadcast identity | Expression stays separate from hitboxes, score, and survival power |

## Tone

The world is absurdly serious, warm, strange, and internally logical. Humor comes from institutions applying total sincerity to serpentine concerns. It does not come from constant puns, random internet references, or characters acknowledging that they are in a joke game.

### Writing rules

- Prefer one precise biological or broadcast image over several snake puns.
- Let each institution have a real belief, useful function, and blind spot.
- Keep critical instruction plain. Flavor follows the instruction or appears at a safe boundary.
- Write from inside serpent culture. Avoid unexplained references to human brands, celebrities, memes, or historical events.
- Keep satire pointed at systems, vanity, certainty, and bureaucracy, not at vulnerable people.
- Use recurring vocabulary consistently and register it by stable content ID.
- Permit silence. A station identity is stronger when it does not comment on every event.
- Keep runtime commentary authored, event-driven, captioned, and bounded. Generative runtime dialogue is outside the 1.0 contract.

### Humor test

A line belongs when it is still in character without the word "snake," reveals an institution or relationship, and does not interrupt play. A line fails when it is only a pun, copies a current meme, explains a rule less clearly, or could be spoken by any station.

## The Serpentine Broadcast Network

The SBN is a shared carrier with eight competing frequencies and an off state. Each station is a cultural institution with a musical rule, worldview, visual system, host voice, and relationship to the Coil. The radio must remain optional. Minimal core cues and visual identity still communicate the world when it is off.

| Station | Institution and origin | Worldview | Broadcast character | Content boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Flow Signal | Ministry of Focus, transmitted from Rhythmos | Sustained flow is a civic practice | Calm, exact, quietly encouraging; host Cadence Vale notices rhythm and recovery | Never scolds failure or turns calm into passivity |
| Chaos Theory | Improvisational Order | Controlled uncertainty prevents cultural stagnation | Curious, sophisticated, playfully analytical; Dr. Sibilant treats risky routes as live composition | Never makes randomness sound like unfair rules |
| The Global Coil | Warm-Blooded Movement and planetary relay collectives | Shared rhythm can create warmth across differences | Communal, kinetic, multilingual in texture; Sol Coil connects local runs to a wider circuit | Never collapses real musical cultures into novelty |
| Ourotron | Order of Retrowave, centered in Ourotron-5 | Imagined futures can be inherited as memory | Romantic, neon, precise; Vektor Null speaks as though lost futures are archived places | Never relies on direct imitation of a real franchise or artist |
| The Pit | Venom Syndicate and mutation engineers | Pressure reveals a signal's true shape | Competitive, physical, ecstatic; DJ Rattlebyte respects bold recovery more than empty aggression | Never masks danger with bass or glorifies an unreadable death |
| The Bureau | Bureau of Information Comfort | Confusion feels safer when delivered with authority | Deadpan civic news led by Anchor Seven, with facts that expose bureaucracy rather than misstate gameplay | Never presents false control, safety, save, or accessibility information |
| The Strike | Molten Core Collective | A clean strike breaks imposed rhythm and makes a new one | Direct, loud, solidaristic; Rivet honors persistence, decisive routing, and refusal to coast | Never becomes generic aggression or contempt for less-skilled players |
| Underground Scales | Thermal Rhythm Division and independent neighborhood relays | Identity is built in the gaps between official signals | Intimate, inventive, lyrical; Molt One treats every shed as an authored signature | Never uses borrowed stereotypes or generic imitation as authenticity |

### Broadcast grammar

Every station receives a finite authored set of:

- Station IDs that establish name, carrier, and point of view.
- Track intros and outros that identify music without talking over critical play.
- Run-start lines keyed to mode, rival, or selected goal.
- Major Vibe Level stingers or compatible layers at declared transitions only.
- Recovery lines keyed to the consumed resource and run context.
- Post-run lines keyed to cause, personal record, rivalry, or tour state.
- Missing-pack and unavailable-audio behavior that stays in character while remaining actionable.
- Accurate captions and text alternatives for every spoken critical cue.

Ordinary combo changes do not cut songs. Host lines use cooldowns, priorities, and a no-repeat bag. Critical warnings duck or interrupt optional broadcast content, never the reverse.

## Factions and productive tension

The world has no single correct ideology. Its depth comes from recurring disagreements that can surface through station copy, rival choices, cosmetics, and optional archive entries.

| Tension | Positions |
| --- | --- |
| Control and improvisation | The Ministry of Focus treats mastery as disciplined rhythm; the Improvisational Order argues that perfect repetition is another form of decay |
| Individual expression and collective signal | Underground Scales protects local identity; the Global Coil emphasizes shared coordination |
| Memory and novelty | Ourotron preserves futures that never arrived; the Strike prefers a decisive break with inherited patterns |
| Safety and transformation | The Bureau minimizes disruption; the Venom Syndicate treats controlled mutation as necessary growth |
| Performance and meaning | The Pit celebrates visible pressure; quieter institutions ask whether spectacle without memory is only noise |

These tensions should create character, not moral alignment bonuses. No faction grants mechanical superiority.

## Let's Play as a broadcast

AI spectator mode should feel like tuning into an active circuit rather than watching a screensaver.

The viewer can:

- Choose a rival, ruleset, seed class, station, or featured matchup.
- Understand the current target, risk band, survival resources, Vibe Level, and record delta at a glance.
- Pause, change speed, step, simplify explanations, or hide the overlay.
- See authored commentary react to typed events without repeating or masking gameplay.
- Challenge the exact seed immediately under identical human rules.
- Follow local rivalries, milestones, and league standings without accounts or fabricated live users.

Automatic qualification runs every rival across the same seed corpus, proves rules equality, detects stalls and loops, measures policy separation, covers commentary triggers and cooldowns, and generates replay evidence. Human validation later judges whether a channel is understandable, entertaining, and worth challenging; its absence does not stop continued automated implementation.

## Broadcast Tour progression

The progression wrapper has the energetic circuit feel of a classic arcade road tour: named rivals, visible standings, authored event cards, strong post-run commentary, and a clear next contest. It does not copy another game's characters, tracks, rules, economy, or presentation.

The Tour is a finite offline path through four broadcast tiers:

1. Local Frequency: teaches clean control, wraps, restart, and the first expression choices.
2. District Relay: introduces power families, route detours, rival identities, and station affiliations.
3. Regional Coil: asks for deliberate synergies, recoveries, stable combos, and seed rematches.
4. Crown Broadcast: combines mastered systems in authored challenges and rival finales.

Each event card declares mode, rules identity, seed policy, featured rival, station context, primary mastery goal, optional style goal, and exact reward. The player may replay any unlocked card, practice without score submission, or return to free play.

Rewards expand expression and world access:

- Authored cosmetic sets and loadout slots.
- Station IDs, approved tracks, host segments, and visual themes.
- Rival dossiers, rematch cards, replay frames, and run-card treatments.
- Archive fragments and contextual notes.
- New challenge configurations that use existing disclosed rules.

Rewards never increase base speed, lives, immunity, score multipliers, spawn luck, or starting power. There are no paid currencies, loot boxes, wait timers, daily chores, streak loss, rotating scarcity, battle passes, or permanent account advantages. A failed attempt preserves knowledge and recorded progress. It never destroys earned access.

The mechanical contract, pacing rules, and save ownership are maintained in [progression](PROGRESSION.md).

## Lore delivery depth

### Surface

Station IDs, rival names, arena labels, power descriptions, achievement text, cosmetic-set names, short commentary, and environmental detail establish the world during normal play.

### Discoverable

Rival dossiers, track notes, station histories, themed set descriptions, tour event context, replay milestones, and recovered broadcast fragments reward curiosity between runs.

### Archive

Long transcripts, institutional histories, timelines, disputed interpretations of the Great Molt, and hidden relationships live in an optional archive. They never interrupt a run or carry required instructions.

## Content record contract

Every authored narrative item needs:

- Stable content ID and schema version.
- Canon tier, speaker, station or faction, and relationship tags.
- Trigger event, priority, cooldown group, repetition policy, and interruption behavior.
- Exact text, caption, localization key, and accessibility alternative.
- Rules, mode, Vibe Level, rival, tour, and spoiler constraints.
- Source, authoring status, rights, file hash, and pack ownership.
- Continuity links to entities, events, and prerequisite entries.

Validators reject unknown speakers, broken continuity IDs, unsafe trigger combinations, missing captions, duplicate IDs, invalid pack ownership, unresolved rights, and lines that exceed their declared UI or timing budget.

## No-waste player trust contract

Vibe Snake maximizes meaningful play, not engagement extraction.

- No microtransactions, paid power, premium currency, gambling, or randomized paid reward.
- No daily obligation, energy meter, wait timer, expiring streak, or fear-of-missing-out schedule.
- No dark-pattern confirmation, disguised advertisement, or fabricated social pressure.
- No grind whose only purpose is inflating time played.
- No content volume claim that hides duplicates, broken files, weak variants, or unclear rights.
- No progression reward that makes a new profile mechanically inferior in comparable play.
- No runtime network requirement for the core game, Tour, replays, AI channels, radio core, or saves.

## World acceptance gate

The world layer is ready for 1.0 only when:

- The minimal offline core communicates the Coil, signal-serpent identity, escalation, and recovery with radio disabled.
- Every station has an approved identity, music boundary, host voice, visual language, event vocabulary, captions, rights, and fatigue review.
- Every final AI rival has measurable policy truth, distinct authored presentation, equal rules, and an immediate seed challenge.
- The Broadcast Tour contains no filler node, hidden mechanical advantage, destructive failure, grind outlier, or manipulative schedule.
- Critical play remains clear when all optional lore and the full radio pack are absent.
- Canon IDs, relationships, triggers, captions, localization keys, rights, and pack ownership pass automatic validation.
- Fixed-seed evidence bundles cover quiet, escalation, recovery, death, restart, rival broadcast, and tour progression under default and accessibility profiles.
- Human evidence remains required for final claims about humor, attachment, station fatigue, rival appeal, and desire to continue, but unavailable human review never stops reversible automated implementation.
